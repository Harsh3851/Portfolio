About

A frontend engineer who is comfortable across the stack.

Since January 2025 I have worked at Ginger Webs on ThinkExam, an enterprise assessment and learning platform. I build Angular 19 applications for administrators, instructors and candidates, and the Laravel REST APIs they depend on, across an LMS, an e-commerce store and a candidate portal.

Recent work includes the live-class module with Zoom integration, an Instructor role with a granular permission model, analytics dashboards and a shared component library. I care about fast first loads, predictable state, accessible components and releases that do not break.
